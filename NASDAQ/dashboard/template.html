Five lightweight AI agents — one school each — were tuned on 2016–2022, then frozen and released onto 2023 → today, years that did not exist in their repository. Each carries a deterministic back-propagation loop: every 63 sessions it receives its own realized errors and adjusts its bounded parameters. The universe is NASDAQ names that pass the AAOIFI sharia screen. One agent gets elected to trade a live virtual account. This page is the ballot count.

Methodology

⚠️ Simulation of algorithmic agents for research. Not investment advice. The AAOIFI screen uses current-day fundamentals (not point-in-time) and a business-activity proxy for the 5% impermissible-income rule; a production screen needs revenue-line data and a scholar review. Universe is today's listings — survivorship bias inflates all backtest numbers. The benchmark is a price index (no dividends).
